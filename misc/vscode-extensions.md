# vscode 插件

> 以下是我在工作学习中常用的插件


## 工具类插件

- [Hex Editor](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=ms-vscode.hexeditor)：内嵌二进制编辑器
- [Draw.io](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=hediet.vscode-drawio)：流程图绘制插件
- [Graphviz Markdown Preview](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=geeklearningio.graphviz-markdown-preview)：支持在 md 文件中显示 graphviz 图
- [Markdown Preview Mermaid Support](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=bierner.markdown-mermaid)：支持在 md 文件中显示 Mermaid 图
- [Todo Tree](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=Gruntfuggly.todo-tree)：在树视图中显示 TODO、FIXME 等注释标记
- [vscode-pdf](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=tomoki1207.pdf)：在 vscode 可以预览 pdf 文件
- [翻译(英汉词典)](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=CodeInChinese.EnglishChineseDictionary)：翻译插件，支持选词翻译
- [GitLens](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=eamodio.gitlens)：git 的图形化插件


## UI类插件

- [Atom One Dark Theme](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=akamud.vscode-theme-onedark)：Atom One Dark UI主题
- [Better Comments](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=aaron-bond.better-comments)：通过关键字高亮注释
- [Error Lens](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=usernamehw.errorlens)：高亮显示代码中存在的错误或警告


## 文件高亮插件

- [GNU Linker Map files](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=trond-snekvik.gnu-mapfiles)：GNU 链接器 .map 文件的语法突出显示和符号列表
